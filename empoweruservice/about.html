About EmpowerUService

Empowering people with disability to live independently and with dignity.

Who We Are

EmpowerUService Living is a registered NDIS provider dedicated to delivering high-quality, person-centred support services. We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to live a fulfilling life, on their own terms.

Our team of experienced support workers and coordinators are passionate about making a real difference in the lives of people with disability across Australia.

Our Mission

To empower individuals with disability by providing compassionate, reliable, and tailored support that promotes independence, choice, and community inclusion.

Our Values

Respect

We treat every person with dignity and honour their choices.

Empowerment

We support people to build skills and confidence to live independently.

Inclusion

We champion community participation and belonging for all.

Excellence

We strive for the highest standard of care in everything we do.
