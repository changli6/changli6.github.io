Our Services

Comprehensive NDIS support tailored to your goals and lifestyle.

EmpowerUService is a values-based organisation providing individually tailored support services to people with disabilities. We offer a full range of critical services across the disability sector and can help you navigate the NDIS process. Below is our complete list of registered NDIS supports.

Jump to a category:

Core Supports Capacity Building Specialist & Clinical Accommodation & Complex

Core Supports

Everyday supports that help you complete daily activities, work towards your goals and participate in the community.

  • Assist — Personal Activities

    Assistance with everyday personal tasks including showering, dressing, eating and mobility, helping you live as independently as possible.

  • Assist — Travel / Transport

    Transport support to help you get to work, school, appointments, community activities and social engagements.

  • Household Tasks

    Help around the home so you can keep your living space safe and comfortable — cleaning, laundry, yard work and more.

  • Participate Community

    Support to take part in community, social, economic and civic activities that matter to you.

  • Group / Centre Activities

    Group-based activities at a centre that build skills, friendships and community connection in a supportive environment.

Capacity Building

Supports that help you build skills and independence so you can reach your long-term goals.

  • Innovative Community Participation

    Creative, individualised ways to help you take part in community life, build networks and reach your personal goals.

  • Development — Life Skills

    Training and support to build practical skills for independent living, such as cooking, money handling, travel and communication.

  • Early Childhood Supports

    Specialist early intervention supports for children under 7 with developmental delay or disability and their families.

  • Support Coordination

    Guidance to understand and use your NDIS plan, connect with providers and coordinate your supports with confidence.

Specialist & Clinical

Higher-intensity and clinical supports delivered by qualified professionals for participants with specialised needs.

  • Assist Personal Activities High

    High-intensity support with personal activities for participants with complex needs, delivered by trained and qualified staff.

  • Behaviour Support

    Specialist behaviour support to reduce the need for restrictive practices and help participants live safely in the community.

  • Community Nursing Care

    Clinical and nursing services delivered in the community, including wound care, medication management and health monitoring.

  • Daily Tasks / Shared Living

    Support with daily household tasks in a shared living arrangement, including cooking, cleaning and budgeting.

Accommodation & Complex Support

Supported accommodation and complex supports for participants with high or specialist needs.

  • Assist — Life Stage, Transition

    Support through key life transitions, such as moving out of home, starting work or study, or changing service providers.

  • Supported Independent Living

    24/7 supported accommodation that empowers you to live as independently as possible. Our SIL homes are safe, comfortable and designed around your needs.

  • Module 2A — Restrictive Behaviour Practices

    Delivery of supports involving restrictive practices, in line with Module 2A requirements, the NDIS Commission and approved behaviour support plans.
